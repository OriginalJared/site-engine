Who this is for

A cordless stick vacuum is ideal for quick daily cleanups and smaller homes. It's light, quick to grab, and easy to use on stairs or in a car. In larger homes with lots of carpet, it often works alongside a full-size vacuum rather than replacing it.

Pet owners should look for tangle-resistant brush rolls. Homes with mostly hard floors benefit from a soft roller or a wet-dry model.

How we picked

  • Cleaning performance: We weighed expert test results on carpet, hard floors, and pet hair over suction specs alone.
  • Runtime: We looked at realistic runtime on standard power, not just the maximum on the lowest setting.
  • Weight and handling: A vacuum that's tiring to hold won't get used, so we compared weight and maneuverability.
  • Filtration and bin: Sealed filtration helps with allergies, and an easy-to-empty bin keeps cleanup less messy.
  • Battery and parts: We favored models with replaceable batteries and easy-to-find parts, which extend a vacuum's life.

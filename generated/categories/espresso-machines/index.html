Who this is for

A home espresso machine pays off if you drink lattes or espresso most days. Manual machines with a steam wand give you the most control but take practice. Automatic and pod machines make a drink at the push of a button.

Budget for a good grinder too. Unless the machine has one built in, a burr grinder makes more difference to espresso than almost anything else.

How we picked

  • Temperature stability: Steady brewing temperature from shot to shot leads to consistent espresso, so we favored machines with PID control or a thermocoil.
  • Grinder: For all-in-one machines, we looked at grind adjustment range and consistency.
  • Milk frothing: We compared manual steam wands and automatic milk systems on how easily they make microfoam for lattes.
  • Ease of use and cleaning: We weighed how much effort daily cleaning and descaling take, especially for automatic machines.
  • Learning curve: We matched each machine to the right user, from beginners who want one-touch drinks to enthusiasts who want control.

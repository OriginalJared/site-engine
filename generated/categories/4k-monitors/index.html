Who this is for

A 4K monitor makes sense if you spend hours looking at text, spreadsheets, code, or photos. At 27 inches, 4K resolution makes text noticeably sharper than a 1440p screen and gives you room to put two windows side by side.

If you mainly play fast competitive games, a high-refresh 1440p monitor is often the better value. If you're plugging in a laptop, look for USB-C with power delivery, which lets one cable handle video, charging, and accessories.

How we picked

  • Panel type: IPS and IPS Black panels hold color and contrast at an angle better than VA and TN panels, which matters for desk work and photo editing.
  • Color accuracy: For creative work we favored monitors that ship factory-calibrated, with a published report or a Calman or Pantone verification.
  • Refresh rate: 60Hz is fine for office work. For gaming we looked for 144Hz or faster, plus a quick response time.
  • Connectivity: USB-C with enough wattage to charge a laptop, a built-in hub, and a KVM switch can replace a separate dock.
  • Value: We compared each monitor's price with the features it actually adds, because many 4K panels are close in image quality.

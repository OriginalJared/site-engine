Who this is for

A robot vacuum is best for keeping floors consistently clean between deeper cleanings, especially with pets or a busy household. It won't replace a stick or upright vacuum for stairs, upholstery, or tight corners.

Self-emptying docks are worth the extra cost if you don't want to empty a small bin every day or two. Mopping models make the most sense in homes with a lot of hard flooring.

How we picked

  • Navigation: Lidar and camera-based mapping lets a robot clean in neat rows, avoid obstacles, and respect no-go zones. We passed over models that bump around at random.
  • Pickup on carpet and hair: Suction numbers alone can mislead, so we also weighed brush design and tangle resistance, which matter most for pet hair.
  • Dock and maintenance: We compared self-empty bag capacity, mop washing and drying, and the ongoing cost of replacement bags and pads.
  • App and obstacle avoidance: Reliable avoidance of cords and socks, plus a clear app with room-by-room control, separates good robots from frustrating ones.
  • Noise and battery: We looked at runtime relative to home size and how loud each robot is on its standard setting.

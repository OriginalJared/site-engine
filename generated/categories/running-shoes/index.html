Who this is for

The best running shoe is the one that feels comfortable on your foot. No single model is right for everyone, so use our picks as a shortlist to try on rather than a final answer.

Daily trainers suit most runners. Stability shoes help if your feet roll inward a lot. Racing shoes with carbon plates are fast but less durable and best saved for race day.

How we picked

  • Comfort and fit: We weighed runner feedback on fit, toe-box room, and comfort over long distances most heavily.
  • Cushioning: We matched how much cushioning each shoe has to the runs it suits, from easy recovery miles to long runs.
  • Stability: For runners who overpronate, we looked at shoes with guide rails or firmer support on the inside of the shoe.
  • Durability: We considered how many miles owners report before the foam or outsole wears down.
  • Weight and drop: We noted each shoe's weight and heel-to-toe drop, since both change how a shoe feels on the run.

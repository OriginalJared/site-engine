Who this is for

A new mattress is worth it if you wake up sore or stiff, or your current bed sags in the middle. Most mattresses last about seven to ten years, and how you sleep matters more than any brand.

Side sleepers usually need a softer surface that cushions the shoulders and hips. Back and stomach sleepers tend to do better on something firmer that keeps the spine level. Couples should weigh motion isolation and edge support.

How we picked

  • Sleeping position and firmness: We matched each mattress's firmness to the sleepers it suits, because the wrong firmness is the most common reason people return a mattress.
  • Construction: Hybrids pair coils with foam for bounce, airflow, and edge support. All-foam beds isolate motion better. We weighed both for different needs.
  • Cooling: Owner feedback on sleeping hot weighed heavily, since dense memory foam can trap heat.
  • Trial and warranty: You can't judge a mattress in a showroom, so we favored at least a 100-night trial and a 10-year or longer warranty.
  • Value: We compared prices at each size and type, because many mid-priced mattresses perform close to luxury models.

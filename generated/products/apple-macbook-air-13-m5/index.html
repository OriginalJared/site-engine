Our Review

The 13-inch MacBook Air is the laptop we'd hand to almost anyone. The M5 chip is far more power than everyday work demands, and like every Apple-silicon Air it has no fan, so it stays completely silent whether you're writing, editing photos, or on a video call. This configuration gets the important things right: 16GB of unified memory and a 512GB SSD, so you won't outgrow it in a year. The 13.6-inch Liquid Retina display is sharp and bright, the 12MP Center Stage camera keeps you framed on calls, and Wi-Fi 7 future-proofs it for newer routers.

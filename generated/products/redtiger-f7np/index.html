Our Review

The REDTIGER F7NP competes directly with the Rove R2-4K at a similar budget price point. The Sony STARVIS 2 sensor delivers sharp 4K footage with excellent night performance, and the included rear camera provides 1080p coverage behind your vehicle. Fast Wi-Fi transfers large files quickly, and built-in GPS logs your routes and speed. The included OBD hardwire kit enables parking mode out of the box. WDR (Wide Dynamic Range) handles harsh lighting transitions like tunnel exits. It's an Amazon's Choice dash cam with proven reliability at scale.

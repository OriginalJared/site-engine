Our Review

The Fully Jarvis Bamboo stands out for two reasons: its eco-friendly bamboo desktop and its exceptional height range. Topping out at 50.7 inches, it's one of the tallest-reaching desks available, making it ideal for users over 6 feet. The dual-motor frame handles 350 lbs with ease, and the bamboo surface is both durable and naturally antimicrobial. Fully offers a 7-year warranty and a 30-day return window. If you care about sustainability and need a desk that reaches higher than most, the Jarvis Bamboo delivers.

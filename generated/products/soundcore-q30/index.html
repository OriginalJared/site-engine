Our Review

The Soundcore Life Q30 have been a budget ANC staple for years. Hybrid ANC with multiple modes adapts to your environment, and the Soundcore app offers a parametric EQ so you can tune the sound exactly — a feature missing from most budget headphones. Up to 50 hours of battery in ANC mode (60 without) means charging twice a month at most. Hi-Res certification over wire and a foldable travel case round it out. Under $60, they're the headphones to beat.

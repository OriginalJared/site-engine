Our Review

The Lectric XP Lite 2.0 is the lightweight, affordable folding e-bike that makes electric riding accessible. It folds down to fit in a car trunk or a small apartment, and at a far lighter weight than the fat-tire XP models it's genuinely easy to carry. The 48V system delivers a peak 819W of power with 5 levels of pedal assist plus a throttle, and the long-range battery option stretches range up to 80 miles. Hydraulic brakes, an LCD display, and a quick-fold frame round out one of the best-selling e-bikes on Amazon.

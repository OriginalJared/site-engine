Our Review

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra is Samsung's everything tablet. The 14.6-inch AMOLED display with 120Hz refresh rate is the largest and most beautiful tablet screen available. The S Pen is included (Apple charges extra), and Samsung's DeX mode transforms the tablet into a full desktop experience with resizable windows. The flagship processor handles any Android app or game without hesitation. for the Wi-Fi model, it's expensive, but you're getting a tablet that genuinely replaces a laptop for most tasks. The dual front cameras make it great for video calls.

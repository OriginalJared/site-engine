Our Review

The Forerunner 265 is the watch serious runners swear by. The morning report greets you with sleep quality, HRV status, training readiness, and weather — everything you need to decide today's workout intensity. Race predictor estimates your 5K, 10K, half marathon, and marathon times based on your training data. The AMOLED display is new for the Forerunner line and looks stunning. 13-day battery in smartwatch mode (20 hours in GPS mode) means you charge it once every two weeks. If you run more than twice a week, this watch will make you faster.

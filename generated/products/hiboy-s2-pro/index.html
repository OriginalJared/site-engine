Our Review

The Hiboy S2 Pro is perfect for riders who never want to deal with flat tires. Its 10" solid honeycomb tires are puncture-proof and maintenance-free, while the 500W motor delivers 19 mph and the battery provides up to 25 miles of range. The rear suspension absorbs bumps that solid tires would otherwise transmit, and the foldable design with app connectivity makes it a practical daily commuter. It's outstanding value for a solid-tire scooter.

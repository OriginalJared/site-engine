Our Review

The Breville Barista Express has been the most recommended home espresso machine for years, and it still earns that title. The built-in conical burr grinder means you're grinding fresh for every shot — the single biggest factor in espresso quality. PID temperature control ensures consistent extraction, and the 15-bar Italian pump delivers proper pressure. The dose-control grinding and manual tamping give you full control over your shots. The steam wand produces microfoam for latte art. It replaces the price daily cafe visits in about 4 months.

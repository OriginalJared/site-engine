Our Review

The Sony WF-1000XM5 are the earbuds audiophiles and reviewers keep coming back to. The Dynamic Driver X delivers deep, textured bass alongside clear mids and highs, and the Integrated Processor V2 drives noise cancellation that rivals over-ear headphones. Battery life reaches about 8 hours with ANC on (24 with the case), and the companion app offers a graphic EQ, LDAC high-res streaming, and adaptive sound control. They're premium-priced, but for pure sound and ANC they set the bar.

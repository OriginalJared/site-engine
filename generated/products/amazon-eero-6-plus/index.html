Our Review

The eero 6+ brings the simplicity of eero to budget-conscious buyers. The 3-pack covers up to 4,500 sq ft with dual-band Wi-Fi 6, and each node includes a built-in Zigbee smart home hub for connecting compatible devices. Setup takes minutes via the eero app, and TrueMesh routing keeps connections stable as you move around the house. It's the easiest way to get whole-home Wi-Fi with smart home integration on a budget.

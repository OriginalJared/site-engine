Our Review

The ASICS Gel-Kayano 31 has been the king of stability running shoes for three decades. The new PureGEL technology in the heel absorbs impact at footstrike, while the FF Blast Plus Eco midsole returns energy on push-off. The 4D Guidance System provides medial support for overpronators without the rigid, heavy feel of traditional stability shoes. It's the shoe that proves stability and comfort aren't mutually exclusive.

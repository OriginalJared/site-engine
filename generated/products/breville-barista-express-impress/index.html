Our Review

The Barista Express Impress adds one critical feature to the beloved Barista Express: automatic tamping. The Impress system doses and tamps with consistent pressure every time — eliminating the most common beginner mistake (inconsistent tamping). An extraction feedback gauge tells you if your shot is running too fast or slow, teaching you to adjust grind size. Everything else matches the original: built-in burr grinder, PID temperature control, 15-bar pump, and steam wand. The higher premium over the original is worth it for the learning assistance.

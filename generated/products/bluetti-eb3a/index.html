The BLUETTI EB3A is the entry point to LiFePO4 portable power., it packs 268Wh of capacity, 600W continuous output, and charges from 0-80% in 30 minutes via wall outlet. It won't run a fridge, but it handles laptops, phones, CPAP machines, drones, and small appliances easily.

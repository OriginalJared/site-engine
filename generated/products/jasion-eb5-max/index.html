Our Review

The EB5 MAX takes Jasion's popular EB5 and adds muscle and practicality. The motor is rated at 2000W peak, the 48V 15Ah (720Wh) battery gives you plenty of range, and 26×4-inch fat tires grip loose ground. What makes it great for daily riding are the extras that usually cost more: cruise control for long flat stretches, fenders to keep you clean, and a rear rack for bags or groceries.

Our Review

The Soundcore Liberty 4 NC are among the most affordable noise-canceling earbuds worth recommending. Adaptive ANC adjusts to your environment in real time, and the 98.5% noise reduction claim holds up well against commuter noise and office chatter. Sound is warm and bass-forward — great for pop and hip-hop — and LDAC support adds high-res streaming on Android. Battery life is excellent at up to 10 hours per charge (50 total), and the Soundcore app includes a full EQ plus a HearID test that personalizes sound to your hearing.

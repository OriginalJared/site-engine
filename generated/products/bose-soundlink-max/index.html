Our Review

The Bose SoundLink Max is Bose's flagship portable speaker, delivering the brand's signature clear, detailed sound at serious volume levels. Dual custom transducers and dual passive radiators produce deep, articulate bass that stays clean even at max volume. The 20-hour battery, IP67 rating, rope handle, and Bluetooth 5.3 with a built-in aux input make it the premium choice for discerning listeners who want the best sound quality in a portable package.

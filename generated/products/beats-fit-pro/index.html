Our Review

The Beats Fit Pro solve the biggest problem with workout earbuds: they stay put. The flexible wingtip locks into your ear and won't budge during running, jumping, or lifting. Powered by Apple's H1 chip, they get active noise cancellation, transparency mode, and spatial audio on both iOS and Android (most Apple-chip earbuds limit Android features). Sound is punchy and bass-forward — ideal for workout playlists — and a quick charge gives an hour of playback in five minutes.

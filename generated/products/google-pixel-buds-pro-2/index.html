Our Review

Google's Pixel Buds Pro 2 are among the most comfortable earbuds you can buy — a smaller, lighter design with a twist-to-adjust fit that stays put during workouts without ear fatigue on long listens. The Tensor A1 chip powers effective noise cancellation and on-device processing, and Gemini integration lets you ask questions, get translations, and control your phone hands-free. Sound is balanced and natural, and Find My Device integration means you'll rarely lose them.

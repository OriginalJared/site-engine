Our Review

Not everyone wants a chunky smartwatch on their wrist. The Fitbit Charge 6 is for people who want health and fitness tracking in a slim, comfortable band they can wear 24/7 without noticing. It tracks heart rate, sleep stages, stress, SpO2, and 40+ exercise types. Google integration means you get Google Maps, Google Wallet, and YouTube Music controls. The 7-day battery means weekly charging, not daily. The built-in GPS tracks outdoor runs and walks without your phone. At $160, it's the best fitness tracker for people who prioritize health data over smartwatch features.

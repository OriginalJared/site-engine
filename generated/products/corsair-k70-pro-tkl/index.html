Our Review

The Corsair K70 PRO TKL is built for competitive gaming. It uses CORSAIR MGX Hyperdrive magnetic (Hall-effect) switches with adjustable actuation and rapid trigger, so you can tune how far a key travels before it registers. The 8000Hz polling rate (8x faster than standard) means keystrokes register in 0.125ms. The TKL layout gives more desk space for mouse movement, and the detachable USB-C cable makes it tournament-portable. Per-key RGB is customizable through iCUE, and the PBT double-shot keycaps won't develop shine. Corsair has been a trusted name in competitive peripherals for over a decade.

Our Review

The Zephyrus G14 packs serious gaming and creative muscle into a laptop that doesn't look or feel like a brick. The GeForce RTX 5080 with 16GB of GDDR7 handles modern games and GPU-heavy editing, while the AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX 370, 32GB of RAM, and 2TB SSD keep everything else fast. The 14-inch 3K OLED at 120Hz is stunning for both games and color work. It's VR-ready, ships with Windows 11 Pro, and has an RGB backlit keyboard.

Our Review

The Vital 200S takes everything good about the Core 300 and adds smart features. The built-in air quality sensor automatically adjusts fan speed based on real-time particle readings, and the VeSync app lets you monitor air quality, set schedules, and control the purifier remotely. It covers up to 1,875 sq ft per hour — enough for open-concept living areas. The washable pre-filter extends HEPA filter life, and the U-shaped air intake maximizes airflow efficiency. Alexa and Google Assistant compatible. At $150, it's the sweet spot between budget and premium.

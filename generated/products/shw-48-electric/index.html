Our Review

The SHW 48-inch is the desk you buy when the budget is tight and you just want to stand. It's the lowest-priced electric standing desk in this roundup, and you feel it in the 110 lb capacity and single-motor lift. But for a laptop and a single monitor, it works. The height range of 27 to 46 inches covers most users, and the desk comes with a basic hook for headphones and a small shelf for peripherals. The 1-year warranty is short, but at this price, the barrier to entry is almost zero.

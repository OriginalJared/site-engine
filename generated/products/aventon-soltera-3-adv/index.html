Our Review

The Aventon Soltera 3 ADV is for riders who want a light, bike-like electric ride rather than a heavy motorcycle-style e-bike. The torque sensor multiplies your pedaling effort so assist feels natural, and the lightweight frame means it still rides well even with the power off. The 250W motor (500W peak) handles commutes and errands with around 50 miles of range, and the hidden battery keeps the look clean. Integrated lighting and a Shimano drivetrain make it a practical, good-looking daily commuter.

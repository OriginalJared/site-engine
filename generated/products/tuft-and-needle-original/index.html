Our Review

The Tuft & Needle Original proved that a great mattress doesn't need to. Their proprietary T&N Adaptive foam is infused with graphite and cooling gel for heat dissipation, and the open-cell structure allows airflow that solid memory foam blocks. It's firmer than most foam mattresses, making it ideal for back and stomach sleepers who need support. It's the best no-frills mattress from a brand you can trust.

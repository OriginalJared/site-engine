Our Review

The Casper Original is the mattress that launched the bed-in-a-box revolution, and it's been refined into a well-rounded choice for almost any sleeper. Breathable foam, zoned support, and a durable base create a balanced medium-firm feel that's soft enough for side sleepers yet supportive enough for back sleepers. It's the safe choice: unlikely to blow your mind, but very unlikely to disappoint.

Our Review

The AULA F75 Pro has been one of Amazon's best-selling mechanical keyboards, and the value proposition is hard to argue with. you get a gasket-mounted 75% layout with a volume knob, hot-swappable switches, pre-lubed Reaper linear switches, RGB backlighting, triple connectivity (BT/2.4GHz/USB-C), and PBT keycaps. The sound profile out of the box is surprisingly good — creamy and thocky without any modifications. The build is plastic, but the typing experience rivals keyboards twice its price. If you want a great wireless mechanical keyboard without spending more, this is it.

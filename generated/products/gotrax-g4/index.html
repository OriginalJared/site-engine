Our Review

The GOTRAX G4 packs impressive specs into a budget-friendly package. Its 350W motor reaches 20 mph, and the 375Wh battery delivers up to 25 miles of range — more than enough for most daily commutes. 10" pneumatic tires provide a comfortable ride, the dual braking system (electronic + rear disc) stops safely, and the foldable design makes it easy to carry on public transit. At under $500, it's the smartest entry into electric scooter commuting.

Our Review

The Nothing Ear (a) pair a distinctive transparent design with performance that punches above their price. The 11mm driver delivers lively, customizable sound, hybrid ANC rated up to 45dB silences commuter noise, and LDAC support brings high-res streaming to Android. Battery life is excellent at up to 9.5 hours per charge (42.5 with the case), and the app offers a detailed EQ plus a Bass Enhance feature. For design-conscious buyers who don't want to spend flagship money, they're hard to beat.

Our Review

The EarFun Air Pro 4 prove you don't need to spend $250 for an excellent set of noise-canceling earbuds. They deliver adaptive hybrid ANC that genuinely quiets commuter noise, Qualcomm Snapdragon Sound with aptX Lossless and LDAC for high-res audio, and a warm, detailed signature that punches well above the price. Battery life is strong at up to 11 hours per charge (52 with the case), and the app offers a full EQ, ANC adjustment, and low-latency game mode. For non-audiophiles, these may be all the earbuds you ever need.

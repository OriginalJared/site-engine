Our Review

TP-Link's Tapo line has quickly become the go-to for budget security cameras, and the C420S1 kit (camera plus hub) is their best value outdoor setup. 2K QHD resolution with a starlight sensor captures usable footage even in near-darkness. The rechargeable battery lasts up to 180 days, and the magnetic mount installs in seconds. Footage stores locally — no subscription needed. The Tapo app is clean and responsive with live view, playback, activity zones, and smart detection. It undercuts Ring and Blink while matching their core features.

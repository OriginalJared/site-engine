Our Review

The Segway Ninebot F2 Pro slots between budget scooters and the flagship Max G2, hitting a sweet spot of price and performance. Its motor reaches 20 mph, and the battery provides an impressive 25+ miles of range. The front suspension fork smooths out bumps, 10" self-sealing tubeless tires grip well and resist punctures, and the Segway app provides ride stats, speed mode settings, and firmware updates. At $649, it's the best Segway for riders who want great range without the Max G2 price tag.

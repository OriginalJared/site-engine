Our Review

The Segway Ninebot F2 Pro slots between budget scooters and the flagship Max G2, hitting a sweet spot of price and performance. Its 400W motor reaches 20 mph, and the 460Wh battery provides an impressive 33 miles of range. The front suspension fork smooths out bumps, 10" pneumatic tires grip well, and the Segway app provides ride stats, speed mode settings, and firmware updates. At $649, it's the best Segway for riders who want great range without the Max G2 price tag.

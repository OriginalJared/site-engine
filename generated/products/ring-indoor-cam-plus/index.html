Our Review

The Ring Indoor Cam (2nd Gen) is the simplest way to monitor inside your home. It delivers 1080p HD video, a physical privacy shutter (flip it closed when you're home), two-way audio, and motion detection. The compact size fits on any shelf or mounts on a wall. Ring's app provides live viewing, event history, and the ability to share clips. It's wired (Micro USB), so no battery concerns. One of the most popular indoor cameras on Amazon with tens of thousands of reviews.

Our Review

The BLUETTI AC200L is the power station you buy when 1000Wh isn't enough but $1,700 for an EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 is too much. 2048Wh of LiFePO4 capacity (expandable to 8192Wh) and 2400W continuous output (3600W power lifting) powers everything from fridges to power tools to window AC units. A 30A RV output and 4 AC outlets cover every scenario, and the BLUETTI app provides Bluetooth monitoring. At $999, it's one of the best capacity-per-dollar buys in the premium tier. 62 lbs means it stays in one place, but that's the tradeoff for this much power.

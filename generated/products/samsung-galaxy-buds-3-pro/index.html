Our Review

If you live in the Samsung ecosystem, the Galaxy Buds 3 Pro are the obvious choice. They auto-switch between your Galaxy phone, tablet, and laptop, and the Galaxy Wearable app offers deep customization including a detailed EQ, 360 Audio with head tracking, and adjustable ANC. The redesigned blade-style stem and new fit improve comfort and call pickup, and the dual-driver setup delivers clean, balanced sound. The SSC Hi-Fi codec enables near-studio-quality streaming from Samsung devices.

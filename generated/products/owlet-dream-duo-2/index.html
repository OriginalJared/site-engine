Our Review

The Owlet Dream Duo 2 combines an HD video monitor with the Owlet Dream Sock — a wearable that tracks your baby's heart rate and oxygen level in real time. If readings fall outside preset ranges, the base station alerts you immediately. The HD camera streams 1080p with night vision, and the Owlet Dream App provides sleep trends and wellness data. For parents who want peace of mind about their baby's vitals, nothing else comes close.

Our Review

The Linksys Atlas Max 6E brings enterprise Qualcomm networking silicon to the home. Each node packs a quad-core Qualcomm processor and tri-band Wi-Fi 6E, with Cognitive Mesh technology that uses machine learning to optimize channel selection, band steering, and client routing. The 3-pack covers up to 9,000 sq ft and handles ultra-fast internet plans. It's the choice for tech-savvy users who want maximum throughput.

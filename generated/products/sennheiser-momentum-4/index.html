Our Review

If sound quality is your top priority, the Sennheiser Momentum 4 wins. Sennheiser has made audiophile equipment for decades, and it shows. The 42mm drivers deliver the most natural, detailed sound of any wireless headphone here — instruments have texture, vocals have presence, and the soundstage is wide. ANC is good (not quite Bose-level, but effective), and 60 hours of battery is extraordinary — charge once every couple of weeks. Premium materials and a comfortable fit make them travel-ready, and at $300 they're a value for audio enthusiasts.

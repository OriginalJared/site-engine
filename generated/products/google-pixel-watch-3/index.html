Our Review

The Pixel Watch 3 is what you get when Google designs both the hardware and software. The circular AMOLED display is gorgeous, Wear OS runs buttery smooth on Google's custom chip, and Fitbit's health tracking (now fully integrated) provides comprehensive wellness data. Google Maps on your wrist is genuinely useful for navigation, and Google Wallet handles payments. The 41mm and 45mm size options fit more wrists than the one-size Galaxy Watch. Battery life hits 24 hours with always-on display — not great, but competitive with Apple Watch.

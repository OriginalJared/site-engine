Our Review

The BenQ PD2706UA is purpose-built for creative professionals, especially Mac users. Its AQCOLOR technology delivers factory-calibrated accuracy (Delta E ≤ 3) covering 99% sRGB and 95% DCI-P3. The USB-C connection with 90W Power Delivery makes it a clean one-cable solution for MacBooks, and it ships with a built-in ergonomic arm. M-Book mode optimizes color rendering to match Apple's display profiles.

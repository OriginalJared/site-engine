Our Review

Most mechanical keyboards scream 'gamer,' but the Razer Pro Type Ultra is built for the office. The silent linear switches have the feel of mechanical keys without the clicky noise that annoys coworkers. The clean white-and-silver design blends into any professional workspace. HyperSpeed wireless and Bluetooth connect to up to 4 devices, and the USB dongle provides a low-latency connection for your primary machine. The ergonomic wrist rest is included, and the white backlight is subtle and functional. Razer Synapse offers full macro programming.

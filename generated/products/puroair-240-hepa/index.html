Our Review

PuroAir has quickly become one of Amazon's top-selling air purifier brands, and the 240 shows why. The built-in PuroIQ air quality sensor automatically adjusts fan speed based on real-time readings, and auto mode keeps your air clean without you thinking about it. Coverage reaches 1,000 sq ft — enough for most living rooms. The certified HEPA filter captures 99.9% of pollutants, and the CarbonTech layer handles odors.

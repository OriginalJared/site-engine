Our Review

The SIHOO M57 is the chair you buy when you need real ergonomic features but can't justify $400+. Adjustable lumbar support, an adjustable headrest, 3D armrests, and a breathable mesh back make it feel like a chair twice its price. The 330 lb weight capacity handles larger users, and the high-back design follows the natural curve of your spine. It's one of the most-reviewed budget ergonomic chairs on Amazon with thousands of positive ratings. It won't last 12 years like a Herman Miller, but for the price it's a legitimate ergonomic chair.

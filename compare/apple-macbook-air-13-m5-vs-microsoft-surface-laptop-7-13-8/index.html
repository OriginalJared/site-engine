Pros and cons

Apple MacBook Air 13"

What we like

  • Fanless and silent under any workload
  • 16GB memory and 512GB storage out of the box
  • Compact and easy to carry
  • Center Stage camera is great for calls

Worth noting

  • Only two USB-C ports
  • No touchscreen
  • 13-inch screen feels tight for side-by-side windows

Microsoft Surface Laptop 7

What we like

  • Long battery life
  • Smooth 120Hz touchscreen
  • 1TB storage
  • Premium build and keyboard

Worth noting

  • A few older Windows apps and games don't run on ARM chips
  • Limited ports
  • Integrated graphics only

Our Review

The Razer Viper V3 Pro pushes the boundaries of competitive gaming mice with a stunning 54g weight and the Focus Pro 35K sensor. With 95 hours of wireless battery life and 8000Hz HyperPolling support, this is Razer's flagship without compromise.

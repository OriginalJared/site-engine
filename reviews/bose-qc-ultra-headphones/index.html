Our Review

Bose wrote the book on noise cancellation, and the QuietComfort Ultra Headphones are its best work. ANC performance is the best available — low-frequency airplane rumble, mid-range office chatter, and high-frequency fan whine all fade away more effectively than with any competitor. Immersive Audio adds spatial depth to music, and CustomTune calibrates the sound to your ear shape on every wear. Up to 24 hours of battery, legendary comfort, and the best ANC make these the headphones for frequent travelers.

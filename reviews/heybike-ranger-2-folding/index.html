Our Review

The Ranger 2.0 solves the biggest e-bike problem: where to put it. The frame folds, so it fits in a car trunk, a closet, or a small apartment, and the step-thru design makes it easy to get on and off. Don't let the folding frame fool you, though. Its 20-inch fat tires handle rough pavement and dirt, the motor is rated at 2000W peak, and the removable 600Wh battery is rated for up to 70 miles.

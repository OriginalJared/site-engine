Our Review

The Roborock Qrevo Curv brings a FlexiArm side brush and AdaptiLift chassis that reach into corners and climb thresholds better than most competitors. With 18,500Pa suction, dual spinning mop pads, and a dock that empties dust, washes mops with hot water, and refills the robot's tank automatically, this is about as close to truly hands-free floor care as it gets. LiDAR navigation maps your home in a single run, and the app lets you set no-go zones, schedule room-specific cleanings, and adjust suction per surface. It undercuts the premium flagships while matching most of their performance.

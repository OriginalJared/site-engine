Our Review

When pure noise cancellation is the priority, Bose still wears the crown. The QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds block more ambient noise than almost any competitor — subway rumble, airplane engines, and office chatter largely disappear. Bose Immersive Audio adds spatial depth to music and podcasts, and CustomTune automatically calibrates the sound and ANC to the shape of your ear. Aware mode with ActiveSense lets voices through while still blocking background noise, making them excellent for travel and the office.

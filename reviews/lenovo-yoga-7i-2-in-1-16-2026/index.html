Our Review

The Yoga 7i folds 360 degrees, so the same machine works as a laptop, a tent for watching shows, or a big tablet for sketching and note-taking. Its 16-inch 2K touchscreen is roomy enough for real work, and the Intel Core Ultra 5 322 includes a 46 TOPS NPU for Copilot+ features. With 16GB of LPDDR5X memory, a 1TB SSD, Windows 11 Pro, a fingerprint reader, and Wi-Fi 7, it's well equipped for school or home.

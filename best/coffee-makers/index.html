Who this is for

A good drip coffee maker is for anyone who brews several cups at a time and wants consistent results without a manual routine. Single-serve machines trade some flavor and cost per cup for speed and convenience.

If you mostly drink one cup and like to tinker, a manual brewer like the AeroPress is cheaper and makes excellent coffee.

How we picked

  • Brewing temperature: Coffee extracts best at about 195–205°F. We favored machines certified by the Specialty Coffee Association or shown to hit that range consistently.
  • Even extraction: A showerhead that wets the grounds evenly and a brew time of about four to six minutes make the biggest difference in taste.
  • Carafe: Thermal carafes keep coffee hot without the burnt taste a hot plate causes over time.
  • Ease of use and cleaning: We looked for simple controls, easy-to-fill reservoirs, and parts that are easy to descale and rinse.
  • Durability and warranty: We weighed owner reports on long-term reliability alongside each manufacturer's warranty.

Who this is for

An air purifier helps if you deal with allergies, pet dander, wildfire smoke, or cooking odors. It works best in the room where you spend the most time, usually a bedroom or living room, running continuously.

Size it to the room. A purifier that's too small for the space will barely change the air no matter what filter it uses.

How we picked

  • CADR and coverage: Clean Air Delivery Rate measures how fast a purifier cleans the air. We matched each unit's CADR to realistic room sizes rather than the optimistic maximum on the box.
  • True HEPA filtration: We favored sealed True HEPA filters, which capture at least 99.97% of particles 0.3 microns in size. We avoided purifiers that depend on ionizers or ozone to clean the air; where a pick has an ionizer, it can be switched off.
  • Noise: A purifier only helps when it's running, so we weighed how quiet it is on a setting that still moves meaningful air, especially for bedrooms.
  • Filter cost: Replacement filters can cost more than the purifier over a few years, so we factored in yearly filter prices.
  • Smart features: Air-quality sensors and an auto mode are useful. App control is a bonus, not a requirement.
